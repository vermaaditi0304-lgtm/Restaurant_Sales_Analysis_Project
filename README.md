# 🍽️ Restaurant Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end analysis of restaurant sales data to uncover valuable business insights related to revenue, customer behavior, product performance, payment preferences, and sales trends.

The project combines **Excel, SQL, Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn), and Power BI** to transform raw transactional data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that supports data-driven decision making.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key business metrics, identify growth opportunities, and make strategic recommendations to improve restaurant performance. 

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall restaurant sales performance.
* Track revenue and order trends over time.
* Understand customer purchasing behavior.
* Identify high-performing products and categories.
* Evaluate payment method preferences.
* Compare sales performance across different cities.
* Analyze customer segments based on age and gender.
* Generate actionable business recommendations using data.

---

# 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Excel** – Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
* **MySQL** – Data Extraction & Business Queries
* **Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn)** – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

# 🔄 Project Workflow

1. Cleaned and prepared the dataset using Microsoft Excel.
2. Imported data into MySQL and performed business queries.
3. Conducted Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) using Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn).
4. Calculated business KPIs and derived key performance metrics.
5. Built a six-page interactive Power BI dashboard.
6. Generated business insights and recommendations based on the analysis.

---

# 📊 Dashboard Overview

The Power BI dashboard consists of **six interactive pages**:

### 1️⃣ Executive Summary

Provides a high-level overview of business performance including:

* Total Revenue
* Total Orders
* Average Order Value
* Unique Customers
* Revenue by Category
* Monthly Revenue Trend

**Key Insights**

* Main Dishes generate nearly **47% of total revenue**, making them the restaurant's primary revenue driver.
* Revenue remains relatively stable across both years with limited year-over-year variation.

---

### 2️⃣ Customer Behavior Analysis

Focuses on customer purchasing patterns.

Includes:

* Top 10 Customers by Revenue
* Customer Segmentation Analysis
* Revenue vs Orders Scatter Plot
* Gender-wise Order Distribution

**Key Insights**

* Revenue is well distributed across customers, reducing dependency on a few high-value customers.
* Higher purchase frequency strongly correlates with higher customer revenue.
* Male and female customers contribute almost equally to total orders.

---

### 3️⃣ Time-Based Sales Analysis

Analyzes sales performance across different time periods.

Includes:

* Revenue by Day of Week
* Revenue by Time of Sale
* Category-wise Revenue by Time Slot

**Key Insights**

* Revenue remains fairly consistent throughout Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and Night.
* Wednesday records the lowest revenue, indicating an opportunity for mid-week promotional campaigns.

---

### 4️⃣ Product & Category Analysis

Evaluates menu performance.

Includes:

* Top 15 Revenue-Contributing Items
* Top 10 Best-Selling Products
* Monthly Revenue by Category

**Key Insights**

* Grilled Chicken, Pasta Alfredo, and Steak contribute over **30% of total revenue**.
* Main Dishes consistently outperform all other categories.

---

### 5️⃣ Payment & City Analysis

Examines geographical performance and payment preferences.

Includes:

* Revenue by Payment Method
* Revenue by City
* Revenue by City & Payment Method
* Geographic Revenue Map

**Key Insights**

* Payment methods are almost evenly distributed among Cash, Credit Card, and Digital Wallet.
* Bangalore generates the highest revenue, while Mumbai records the lowest.

---

### 6️⃣ Age Group Revenue Analysis

Analyzes customer demographics.

Includes:

* Revenue by Age Group
* Orders by Age Group
* Category-wise Revenue Across Age Groups

**Key Insights**

* Customers aged **36–45** generate the highest revenue.
* Customers aged **18–25** contribute the least, highlighting a potential growth opportunity.
* Main Dishes remain the highest revenue-generating category across every age group.

---

# 📈 Key Business Insights

* Main Dishes are the restaurant's largest revenue contributor.
* Revenue is consistently distributed across different times of the day.
* Customer revenue increases with purchase frequency.
* Payment preferences are balanced across all available payment methods.
* Bangalore is the strongest-performing city.
* Working professionals (36–45 years) contribute the highest revenue.
* Revenue is diversified across customers, reducing business risk.
* Top-selling menu items account for a significant portion of total revenue.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

* Bundle Main Dishes with Drinks or Side Dishes to increase Average Order Value.
* Introduce premium beverages to improve revenue from the Drinks category.
* Launch seasonal promotions and limited-time offers to boost sales.
* Implement customer loyalty and reward programs to increase repeat purchases.
* Introduce Wednesday combo offers to improve mid-week sales.
* Promote high-performing menu items while improving the visibility of lower-selling products.
* Increase marketing efforts in lower-performing cities such as Mumbai.
* Target the 18–25 age group through student discounts, cashback offers, social media campaigns, and modern dining experiences.
